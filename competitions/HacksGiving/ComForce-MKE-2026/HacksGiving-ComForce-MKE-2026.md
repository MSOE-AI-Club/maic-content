## Technology for Safer Communities

[ComForce MKE](https://comforcemilwaukee.com/), the [MSOE AI Club](https://msoe-maic.com/), and MSOE are hosting **Hacksgiving 2026: AI for Good**. Over one weekend, teams will build a working prototype that helps ComForce MKE and its partners turn fragmented information into coordinated action and measurable community impact.

The event starts with a kickoff on **Thursday, November 19, 6:30–7:30 PM** and ends with finals and presentations on **Sunday, November 22, 11:00 AM–3:00 PM**, both in MSOE Diercks Hall.

You do not need prior experience in community violence prevention or AI. You do need a team that is willing to learn from the people doing this work and build something they could actually use.

<details open style="border: 1px solid #64748b; border-radius: 10px; padding: 14px 18px; margin: 22px 0;">
  <summary style="cursor: pointer; font-size: 1.5rem; font-weight: 700;">The challenge</summary>
  <div style="margin-top: 14px;">
    <p>Community violence prevention depends on timely information, effective coordination, and strong relationships between organizations and the people they serve. In Milwaukee, organizations like ComForce MKE work directly with community members to prevent violence, mediate conflicts, respond to crises, support families, and connect individuals with essential resources. This also rings true with responding to mental health crises, auto accidents, house fires, missing persons, and disaster-related relief, which at times intersect with the CVI/first response work.</p>
    <p>This work takes place within a complex ecosystem of nonprofit organizations, healthcare providers, government agencies, law enforcement, and community advocates. Although these organizations share a commitment to safer communities, they often operate with disconnected information systems, limited resources, and challenging communication processes.</p>
    <p>The consequences are significant. Frontline workers may lack information needed to respond effectively. Requests for assistance and referrals can be difficult to coordinate and track. Organizations may struggle to document their activities, retain access to their own data, and demonstrate their impact to funders and community partners.</p>
    <p style="font-size: 1.15rem;"><strong>Your challenge: How can technology help ComForce MKE and its partners turn fragmented information into coordinated action and measurable community impact?</strong></p>
  </div>
</details>

<details open style="border: 1px solid #64748b; border-radius: 10px; padding: 14px 18px; margin: 22px 0;">
  <summary style="cursor: pointer; font-size: 1.5rem; font-weight: 700;">What we're looking for</summary>

Your goal is to develop a working prototype that addresses a specific, real-world challenge faced by ComForce MKE or its community partners. Solutions may involve web or mobile applications, data analytics, visualization, artificial intelligence, or other technologies. We encourage creativity and technical ambition, but the most successful projects will combine innovation with practical usefulness.

As you develop your solution, consider:

- **Community Impact:** How does your solution improve the ability of organizations to serve Milwaukee residents?
- **Practical Implementation:** Could a community-based nonprofit realistically adopt, maintain, and use your solution?
- **Accessibility and Usability:** Can frontline workers and community members use it easily?
- **Responsible Technology:** Does your solution protect sensitive information, respect individual privacy, and preserve human judgment in consequential decisions?

**Projects should avoid identifying or predicting individuals as potential perpetrators of violence and should use only appropriately authorized, de-identified, or synthetic data.**

### The Bigger Picture

Hacksgiving is an opportunity to apply technology to challenges that matter beyond the classroom. We encourage you to engage with ComForce MKE, understand the experiences of the people doing this work, and develop solutions grounded in their needs.

The best technology doesn't just organize information or automate tasks. It helps people work together more effectively, make better decisions, and create meaningful change in their communities.

### How presentation order works

- **Teams present in the order they submit.** The first team to submit presents first, the second team presents second, and so on.
- **Everyone on your team gets an email** as soon as your team submits. It tells you what number you are presenting and which team presents right before you, with their team name and member names, so you know when to get ready.
- **Your spot is locked in at your first submission.** If you update your GitHub link before the deadline, you keep the same spot.
- **Check your spam or junk folder** if you don't see the email within a few minutes. It comes from "MSOE AI Club via ALL Network" and goes to the email on your ALL AI Network account.

### The presentation

- **Presentations are limited to 4 minutes per team.** Time limits will be strictly enforced; teams that exceed the limit will be stopped by the judges.
- **Key understanding:** being able to effectively explain how your solution works to non-technical audiences will aid your chances of becoming an award winner.
- **Live demo:** if you believe a live demo would aid in your explanation, feel free to include one. However, live demos should not exceed 2 minutes of the presentation.
- **Not every team member needs to present.**
- Your team's placement will be based on what you share during the presentation.

### Rules

- You are not allowed to present projects that were built before Thursday, November 19, 2026.
- You are allowed to use open-source LLMs, external APIs, and existing packages that ComForce MKE could reasonably use.
