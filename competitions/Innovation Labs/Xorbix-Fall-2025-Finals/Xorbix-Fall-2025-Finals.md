## Innovation Labs Fall 2025: AI Solutions with Xorbix & Databricks

**Innovation Labs Fall 2025**, hosted by the MSOE Artificial Intelligence Club (MAIC) in collaboration with Xorbix Technologies, Inc. and Databricks, wrapped up an incredible 2-month program where 180+ students built AI-powered solutions for real industry challenges.

<img src = './images/thumbnails/Xorbix-Technologies-logo-profile.jpg' height = 100px>
<br/>

## What Students Built
Beginning in October, students from Milwaukee School of Engineering, Rose-Hulman Institute of Technology, Pasadena City College, University of Wisconsin-Milwaukee, and Marquette University worked with their peers, student mentors, and industry mentors from Xorbix to build projects across manufacturing, health, and insurance operations.

Teams explored real-world problems like product defect detection, fraud detection, energy efficiency, and more, using AI to turn complex workflows into practical, deployable solutions.

$6,000 in prizes recognized the top teams for building solutions with strong technical depth, creativity, and real-world impact.

- **3rd Place – Xorvengers**: Travis Jankowski, Rossi Doyle, Alexander Lopez, Gabrielle Sladek, Liam Salas, Zachary Burke, Cade Rousseau, Nikolas Palacios, Patrick R. — For their defect detection system deployed on mobile, web, and virtual reality to tackle a widespread problem in many domains.
